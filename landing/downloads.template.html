A quick note before you open it.

This early-access release is unsigned. Windows or macOS may ask you to approve the installer or app.

On Windows, SmartScreen may offer More info → Run anyway. On macOS, after trying to open the download, check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Your device’s policy may restrict these options.
