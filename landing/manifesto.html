AxiomAI · Manifesto · MMXXVI

Liberty. Privacy. Sovereignty.

Thinking has always been free. You could sit alone and work through an idea without censorship, without asking permission, and without anyone storing a record of it.

AI changed how and where we think. What once happened inside your head now happens in a chat window that belongs to someone else. The question you are not ready to say aloud, the draft you would never send, the idea that still sounds strange even to you—type it in, and it passes through servers you do not control.

At AxiomAI, we do not believe thinking should carry that risk. It should be free, private, and yours to control.

But AI is moving beyondthought. It is beginning to act.

The systems we use to write and reason will soon manage assets, purchase services, execute strategies, and negotiate with other machines. An agent will know what you want and be able to do something about it. When that happens, privacy can no longer end at the chat window. It must extend to the agent's memory, tools, money, and actions.

That is the future we're building AxiomAI for.

I · Libertas

Liberty

Liberty is the right to use your own mind freely, without having the boundaries of acceptable thought set by a company, platform, or jurisdiction you had no role in choosing.

Today, that freedom changes depending on which AI product you use. What you can ask, what gets retained, and who may eventually see it depends on the provider, the country you live in, and the fine print nobody reads. The tool may feel personal, but the terms of the relationship aren't.

As AI becomes agentic, liberty also means deciding how that intelligence may act for you. If you create an agent to write code or trade, you should be able to set its objectives, its tools, and the limits of its authority.

An agent should not be freefrom rules. It should be governedby those you set for it.

II · Secretum

Privacy

Liberty is our foundational principle. Privacy is how we put it into practice.

Most AI companies handle privacy with a promise: trust us not to misuse your data. That promise must survive every employee, internal system, acquisition, subpoena, and breach. One failure anywhere in the chain can compromise the whole arrangement.

AxiomAI approaches privacy architecturally. Messages are encrypted before leaving the user's device and processed inside protected execution environments, preventing the surrounding infrastructure from reading them. The goal is to reduce how much trust privacy requires, because a promise is always weaker than system design.

Private inference is only the beginning.

An agent can keep its reasoning confidential and still reveal its owner through its behavior. The assets it holds reveal its position. The markets it enters reveal its strategy. Its counterparties reveal its relationships. The timing of a transaction can disclose its intent.

This is why private AIneeds private money.

If an agent's reasoning is protected but every economic action is public, the intelligence behind it can be reconstructed by its footsteps.

Luckily, there is an existing ecosystem built for private money. Zcash gives agents a way to hold and move value without making every balance, payment, and relationship public by default. For AxiomAI, building with Zcash supports a larger idea: an agent should preserve its owner's privacy not only while thinking, but while doing useful work.

Privacy should not end once AIs take action. If anything, once AIs start moving your money, the standard of privacy should be even higher.

III · Imperium

Sovereignty

Privacy asks who can read your information. Sovereignty asks who controls the system acting on your behalf.

An agent with authority to transact cannot be governed by vague instructions and retrospective assurances. Its owner needs to define what it may spend, which assets it may use, what markets it may enter, and when its authority begins and ends. Those limits must be enforceable without requiring the owner to watch every action.

They must also be verifiable.

An agent should be able to keep its strategy private without becoming unaccountable to the person who authorized it. Its owner may not inspect every decision, but should still be able to establish that the agent stayed within its permissions.

This is where AxiomAI and Astrea meet. AxiomAI is where users create agents, give them tools, and define their authority. Astrea is the environment through which those agents act, with permissions checked and accepted activity recorded in a form that can be verified. Zcash provides private money and settlement. Ethereum provides access to programmable assets, markets, and liquidity.

Sovereignty will be built in stages: private conversations, then private memory and strategy, then private money and economic activity. Over time, more of the relationship between a user and an agent must move out of the provider's control and into the user's own.

We are not there yet.But the path to sovereigntystarts with privacy.

AxiomAI is live today, private by default, and being built toward a future in which AI does more than think with you. It acts for you while remaining answerable to you.
