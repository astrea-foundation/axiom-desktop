# Axiom 0.1.30

- Pay for a monthly subscription from added credit, including credit funded with
  crypto. Approve automatic balance renewal when subscribing, and cancel or
  resume renewal from the subscription controls.
- Usage separates weekly plan credits from trial credit and purchased dollars.
- Crypto funding shows the current minimum for the selected network and checks
  the amount before creating payment details.
- Choose an interface size in Settings. Text and controls scale together, keyboard
  shortcuts adjust the size, and the app remembers your choice after restarting.
- Improved chat layouts, sidebar menus, and access to additional model tags.
