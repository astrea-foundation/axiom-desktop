# Axiom 0.1.29

- **Thinking** opens automatically while reasoning streams. It collapses as the
  model starts its answer or a tool call and shows the recorded duration.
- The model picker shows one entry per model. New chats use the best available
  offering for that model, and existing chats retain their provider.
- When free trials are at capacity, the notice gives a retry time in your local
  time zone and offers a shortcut to plans.
