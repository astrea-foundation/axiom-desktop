# Axiom 0.1.14

The 0.1.14 candidate did not reach publication. Its changes are included in
[0.1.15](0.1.15.md).

This release adds six-hour crypto invoices with payment countdowns, server-side
cancellation and immutable quote refresh. Accounts may have five active invoices;
cancelling one makes room for another. The client preserves invoice deadlines
across quote refreshes and supports checking late payments.

Free-trial capacity errors show when to retry. Requests admitted before the
shared trial budget reaches capacity may finish, and paid credit remains usable.
The billing contract accepts the matching backend's trial-capacity and invoice
metadata and exposes invoice actions through Desktop and AxiomCLI.

The release retains the Electron packaging correction for shared chat validation
and all features from [0.1.13](0.1.13.md). Desktop and its bundled CLI share the
0.1.14 product version.

Roll out with the compatible platform backend after applying its
`platform_20261012_capacity` migration and runtime grants. Production publication
requires successful exact-source CI, signed installers and final-byte verification.
Signed-package and interactive qualification requirements remain in
[release acceptance](../validation.md).
